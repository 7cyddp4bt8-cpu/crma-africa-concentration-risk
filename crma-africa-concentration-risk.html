The CRMA's own Article benchmark prohibits more than 65% reliance on any single country for any strategic raw material. Of the 7 key minerals above, 5 already breach this threshold at the mining or refining stage. The EU's African partnerships deepen concentration in DRC and South Africa — the same minerals, the same countries, with no multilateral backstop if either counterparty restricts exports. The DRC exercised exactly this leverage in February 2025 with a cobalt export ban, extended twice. The architecture failed its own stress test before it was fully built.
